## 1. Welcome to WealthOS

WealthOS is designed around a single guiding principle: **Powerful internally. Simple externally.**

You do not need to be an accountant, chartered financial analyst, or software engineer to understand your money. Behind every screen, WealthOS maintains a professional double-entry accounting ledger that tracks every rupee with mathematical precision, while presenting you with clear, intuitive answers to your financial questions.

## 3. The 5 Questions Every User Must Know

When you visit your **Overview** (`/dashboard`), the executive banner immediately answers the 5 essential questions of personal finance:

1. **Total Net Worth** (`ACTUAL`): What you own minus what you owe across all accounts.
2. **What I Own** (`ACTUAL`): Your total assets, broken down into liquid cash for immediate use versus long-term investments.
3. **What I Owe** (`ACTUAL`): Your outstanding loans and credit cards, alongside your solvency buffer ratio.
4. **Net Cash Flow** (`ACTUAL`): Your monthly incoming money minus your outgoing expenses, showing your net savings rate.
5. **Goals Progress** (`PROJECTED`): How close you are to achieving your funded financial milestones.

---

## 4. Understanding Data Badges: `ACTUAL` vs `PROJECTED`

To ensure you can always trust the numbers you see:
- **`ACTUAL` (Green Badge)**: Indicates a confirmed, settled fact verified against your double-entry accounting ledger or bank statement.
- **`PROJECTED` (Blue Badge)**: Indicates a forward-looking simulation, inflation-adjusted forecast, or actuarial retirement estimate.

## 5. Collaborating in a Household Workspace

Through the **Household** module (`/household`), you can manage wealth together with family members or partners:
- **Privacy First**: You control exactly what is shared (`PERSONAL_ONLY`, `HOUSEHOLD_SHARED`, or `LIMITED_SHARED`).
- **Zero Double Counting**: Shared accounts are factored into your combined household net worth according to agreed ownership percentages.
- **Internal Settlements**: When one member pays for another, internal settlements track reimbursements without distorting your income or spending totals.
