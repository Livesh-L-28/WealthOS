# WealthOS Phase 19 — Deterministic Financial Simulation Engine

## 1. Monte Carlo & Decision Simulation Invariants
1. **No Speculative Growth Guarantees**: Simulations model distributions with documented standard deviation and inflation adjustments; never marketing guaranteed returns.
2. **Explicit Cash Flow Modeling**: Forecasts integrate verified monthly recurring EMIs, utility burn, and SIPs as fixed constraints.
3. **Worst-Case Stress Testing**: Simulations test historical stress scenarios (e.g., 2008 GFC, 2020 COVID contraction, stagflation) to evaluate emergency fund resilience.
